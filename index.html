    Adnan Ali, Ph.D.

    Postdoctoral Researcher at Jinan University, China

    Graph Learning · Representation Learning · 3D Scene Representation

    I am a researcher working on machine learning and representation learning for structured and visual data.

    My established research focuses on graph neural networks, graph contrastive learning, self-supervised learning, adaptive data augmentation, negative sample selection, and robust representation learning.

    My current research is expanding toward 3D scene representation and computer vision, particularly 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) and its extension toward semantic 3D understanding.

    Current Research

    Graph Representation Learning

    My research in graph learning investigates how useful and robust representations can be learned from graph-structured data through self-supervised and contrastive learning.

    Current and previous topics include:

    • Graph Contrastive Learning
    • Graph Neural Networks
    • Self-Supervised Learning
    • Adaptive Data Augmentation
    • Negative Sample Selection
    • Robust Representation Learning

    3D Scene Representation

    I am currently exploring 3D Gaussian Splatting as an efficient representation for 3D reconstruction, rendering, and scene understanding.

    My current work includes:

    • 3D Gaussian Splatting
    • 3D Reconstruction
    • COLMAP-based Scene Preparation
    • Rendering and Visualization
    • Reconstruction Evaluation
    • Semantic 3D Representation

    Research Notes & Tutorials

    Research involves much more than final experiments and publications. It also involves installation problems, implementation details, useful commands, debugging, experimental decisions, and solutions that are often difficult to remember later.

    I maintain a public research notebook where I document these experiences as part of my normal research workflow.

    The notes primarily serve as a reference for myself, but I publish them openly so they may also help researchers and students working on similar problems.

    Current Notebook Areas

    • 3D Gaussian Splatting — installation, training, rendering, COLMAP, SIBR, custom datasets, and troubleshooting
    • Graph Learning — graph neural networks, contrastive learning, negative sampling, and experimental notes
    • Research Computing — Python, Conda, CUDA, Git, LaTeX, and research environments
    • Chinese Learning — vocabulary, expressions, academic Chinese, and practical notes from continuing my Mandarin studies
