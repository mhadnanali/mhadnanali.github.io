How to interpret this demonstration

What is physically meaningful?
At a pixel, Gaussians have different effective α values and are composited front-to-back. The rendered feature is a weighted sum; error gradients reach the participating Gaussian features.

What is simplified? We model just one pixel, two Gaussians, three channels, zero background features, and basic SGD rather than the actual multi-view, 256-channel, CUDA/Adam training pipeline.

Crucial: Compositing weights do not have to sum to 1. The remainder is background transmittance.

Synthetic demonstration, not features or rendering weights measured from the user's indoor-scene models. Gaussian feature numbers may be signed and are not RGB colors. Effective opacity is shown as αᵢ(p) = opacityᵢ × exp(−½d²ᵢ(p)).
