## Publication Profile

My published work has developed from earlier research on **vehicular delay-tolerant networks, security, natural-language understanding, and social-network analysis** toward a stronger focus on **graph representation learning and graph contrastive learning**.

My recent first-author work investigates adaptive data augmentation, negative sample selection, robustness, and stability in graph contrastive learning.
