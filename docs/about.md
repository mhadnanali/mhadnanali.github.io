# About

I am **Adnan Ali, Ph.D.**, currently working as a **Postdoctoral Researcher at Jinan University, China**. My research interests span machine learning, representation learning, graph learning, and 3D scene representation.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from the **University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)**. My doctoral research focused on graph contrastive learning for node representation and classification, with particular emphasis on data augmentation, negative sample selection, robustness, and adaptive learning strategies.

My current research is expanding toward **3D scene representation and computer vision**, particularly **3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)** and its potential extensions toward semantic 3D understanding.

My broader research interests include graph neural networks, self-supervised learning, robust representation learning, computer vision, multimodal learning, and intelligent systems. I have also worked on problems involving data mining, social-network analysis, speech recognition, natural-language processing, cybersecurity, and applied machine learning.

## Chinese Language Learning

## This Website

This website serves two main purposes.

First, it is my **academic research profile**, presenting my current research directions, publications, projects, and research interests.

Second, it is my **public research and learning notebook**, where I preserve practical tutorials, implementation notes, experimental workflows, technical solutions, and other useful knowledge encountered during my research and learning.

Rather than maintaining a separate technology blog, I document useful knowledge naturally as part of my research workflow. These notes primarily serve as a reference for myself, while also being openly available to researchers, students, and learners who may encounter similar problems.
