# Research

My research focuses broadly on **representation learning**, with particular interest in learning robust and useful representations from structured and visual data.

## Graph Representation Learning

A major part of my research has focused on graph neural networks and graph contrastive learning.

During my Ph.D., I investigated several challenges in self-supervised graph representation learning, including:

- graph data augmentation
- negative sample selection
- adaptive training strategies
- robustness and stability
- node representation learning

My Ph.D. thesis focused on essential techniques of graph contrastive learning for node classification.

### Current Graph Learning Work

My current work in this direction includes **AdNGCL**, an adaptive graph contrastive learning framework that controls the exposure of negative samples during representation learning.

The framework separates the negatives available to an anchor from those actively participating in the contrastive objective and uses adaptive scheduling to regulate their contribution during training.

## 3D Scene Representation

My current research is also expanding toward **3D scene representation and computer vision**.

I am particularly interested in:

- 3D Gaussian Splatting
- 3D reconstruction
- neural rendering
- scene representation
- semantic 3D understanding
- representation learning for visual environments

### 3D Gaussian Splatting

I am currently working with the 3D Gaussian Splatting pipeline from data preparation and camera reconstruction through model training, rendering, visualization, and quantitative evaluation.

A longer-term objective is to investigate how learned semantic information can be incorporated into Gaussian-based scene representations.

## Research Direction

Although graph learning and 3D scene representation operate on different forms of data, both directions are connected by a common question:

**How can we learn compact, robust, and useful representations of complex structured information?**

My broader research interests include self-supervised learning, robust machine learning, multimodal learning, and intelligent systems.
