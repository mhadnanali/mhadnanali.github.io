What should you notice?
Both camera views refer to one trainable Gaussian vector — there are not two independent copies of it.
All values, chair diagrams and camera contributions are synthetic teaching examples. This lab models one selected Gaussian at one pixel per view with fixed contributions from other layers, and uses L1 subgradient descent. Real Feature 3DGS optimizes a whole scene using many pixels and views, 256-D Gaussian features, differentiable rasterization and additional RGB reconstruction supervision.
